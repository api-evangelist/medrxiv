Importance Public prostate cancer data may appear under different repository identifiers or releases while representing overlapping patients, specimens, or sample records. The implications for reported analytic independence require assessment at both resource and publication levels. Objective To characterize supported nonindependence among selected public prostate cancer resources and assess reported overlap control in publications using related resources.
