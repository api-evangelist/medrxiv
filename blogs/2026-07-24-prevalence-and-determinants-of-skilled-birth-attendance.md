Background Skilled birth attendance is essential to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality. However, the use of skilled delivery services is still low in many rural areas of Ghana including the Sene East District. This study investigated the level and determinants of skilled birth attendance among women who delivered in the district in the last 12 months.
