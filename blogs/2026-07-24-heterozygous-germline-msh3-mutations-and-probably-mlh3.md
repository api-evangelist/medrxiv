Background. MSH3 and MLH3 are non-canonical DNA mismatch repair genes, involved in repairing insertion-deletion mutations. Colorectal cancer (CRC) and adenomas have been reported in patients with bi-allelic germline MSH3 mutations, and in a very few bi-allelic MLH3 mutation carriers.
