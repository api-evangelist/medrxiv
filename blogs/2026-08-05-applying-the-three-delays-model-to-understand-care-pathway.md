Abstract Background. Outcomes for low-birth-weight (LBW) neonates depend not only on biology but on the timeliness of the care pathway. The Three Delays Model--deciding to seek care (Delay 1), reaching the hospital (Delay 2), and receiving adequate care after arrival (Delay 3)--offers a validated lens for locating where that pathway fails.
