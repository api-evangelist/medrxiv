Background: North Carolina Medicaid implemented the Pregnancy Medical Home program to improve access to high-quality maternity care and reduce the risk of adverse perinatal outcomes. Program recipients receive a prenatal risk screening form, originally intended to identify those at high risk of preterm birth and low birth weight, that includes an assessment of social and clinical factors.
