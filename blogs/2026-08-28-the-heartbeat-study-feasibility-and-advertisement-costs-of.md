Background: Recruitment of diverse participants remains a challenge in cardiovascular clinical trials. Little is known about how recruitment efficiency and advertising costs with web-based tools vary across US communities. We evaluated an online recruitment platform and examined the cost of acquiring both all-comers and diverse participants in relation to community-level income.
