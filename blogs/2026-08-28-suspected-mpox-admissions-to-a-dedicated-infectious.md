Mpox has re-emerged as a public-health priority across Nigeria, and successive international public-health emergencies were declared in 2022 and 2024. Facility-level descriptions of admitted, clinically suspected cases from northeastern Nigeria remain sparse. We conducted a register-based descriptive study of all admissions to the infectious-diseases isolation ward of Modibbo Adama University Teaching Hospital, Yola, in which mpox was recorded as the working or a differential diagnosis between February 2022 and January 2025.
