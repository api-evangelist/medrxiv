Background: Apical sparing of left ventricular longitudinal strain (LS) is an echocardiographic clue to cardiac amyloidosis but may also occur in hypertensive heart disease (HHD). Objectives: To determine whether apical sparing in HHD is associated with regional left ventricular wall stress estimated according to Laplace's law. Methods: We retrospectively studied 1,559 patients with HHD, 47 with light-chain cardiac amyloidosis (ALCA), and 409 normotensive controls.
