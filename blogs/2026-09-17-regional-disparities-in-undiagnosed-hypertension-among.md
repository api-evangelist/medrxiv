Background South Asians (from Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, the Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka) experience a disproportionately high burden of cardiovascular disease, with hypertension serving as a major modifiable risk factor. Despite this elevated risk, hypertension often remains underdiagnosed, particularly in community settings with limited access to routine screening.
