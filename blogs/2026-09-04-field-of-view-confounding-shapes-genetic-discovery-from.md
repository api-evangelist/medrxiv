Self-supervised models increasingly convert medical images into quantitative phenotypes for biological discovery, but statistical reproducibility does not establish that a learned phenotype represents the intended anatomy. We trained a video masked-autoencoder on 69,932 UK Biobank cardiac cine-MRI studies and performed genome-wide association analysis of its latent representation.
