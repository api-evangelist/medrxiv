Short AbstractEmerging evidence indicates that oral and systemic health are interconnected, yet the basis of this relationship remains incompletely understood. In a genome-wide association study of objectively measured dental caries in permanent dentition among Danish children and adolescents (DCCA) (N = 151,521), we identified 14 independent loci. Genes at DCCA-associated loci were enriched for expression in immune, secretory and epithelial cell populations.
