Electronic health records (EHR) are increasingly recognised as critical digital infrastructure for integrated, patient-centred care in the context of rising multimorbidity. In low-resource settings, national EHRs may also support locally driven learning to improve adaptive care across chronic conditions. However, there is limited empirical evidence on whether and how these systems enable learning within routine care in ways that inform broader system adaptation.
