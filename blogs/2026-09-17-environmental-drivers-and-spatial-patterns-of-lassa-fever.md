Lassa fever is a major yet persistently neglected viral hemorrhagic disease in West Africa, with an estimated 100,000-300,000 infections and approximately 5,000 deaths reported annually across the region. Case-fatality rates are typically above 15% among hospitalized patients and may approach 50% during epidemic periods, with Nigeria accounting for the largest reported burden of confirmed cases. This study presents a pilot multi-layer GIS framework for the spatial recording, visualization, and analysis of hemorrhagic fever infections in Nigeria, focusing primarily on Lassa fever.
