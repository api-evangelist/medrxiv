Background: As medicine grows increasingly technological and scientific, biomedical researchers working to bring new knowledge to bear on clinical practice face a key question: when is a new intervention or treatment ready for clinical use? Because this is both a technical and ethical dilemma, it is crucial to examine the social history of how different approaches to this question emerge and the values or assumptions they embed.
