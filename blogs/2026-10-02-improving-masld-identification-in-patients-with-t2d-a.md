Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) is highly prevalent among individuals with type 2 diabetes (T2D), yet accurate identification from electronic health records (EHRs) remains challenging because clinically relevant information is fragmented across structured data and unstructured clinical documentation. We developed a rule-based computable phenotype (CP) framework that integrates structured EHRs with clinical notes for scalable MASLD identification in patients with T2D.
