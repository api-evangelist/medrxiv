Objective: Regression-based normative approaches are widely used in neuropsychology but often rely on score transformations to satisfy model assumptions. We compared previously published linear regression (LR)-based norms with norms derived using Generalized Additive Models for Location, Scale and Shape (GAMLSS) for the brief cognitive battery used in the Norwegian Dementia Disease Initiation (DDI) cohort.
