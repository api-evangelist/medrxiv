Abstract Routine blood tests can be used to calculate several inflammation-related ratios, including the neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR), systemic immune-inflammation index (SII), and platelet-to-lymphocyte ratio (PLR). These indices are widely reported as markers of cardiovascular risk, but it is unclear whether they provide comparable information or whether SII, which is derived from platelet count and NLR, adds anything beyond NLR.
