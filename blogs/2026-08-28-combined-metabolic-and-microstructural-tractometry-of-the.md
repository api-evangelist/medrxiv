Introduction The superior longitudinal fasciculus (SLF) is a major association fiber bundle implicated in cognition, visuospatial attention, language, and motor control, and its impairment is linked to several neurological and neuropsychiatric disorders. This proof-of-concept study was performed with three main objectives in healthy adults.
