Aphasia diagnosis in Indonesia remains challenging due to limited culturally and linguistically appropriate instruments. Widely used tools such as the Boston Diagnostic Aphasia Examination (BDAE) and Western Aphasia Battery (WAB) are not adapted to the Indonesian context, while Tes Afasia untuk Diagnosis, Informasi, dan Rehabilitasi (TADIR) provides screening but lacks diagnostic accuracy.
