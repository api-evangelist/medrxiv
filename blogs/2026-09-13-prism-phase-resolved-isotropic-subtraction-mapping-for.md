Multi-phase contrast-enhanced computed tomography (CT) is the gold standard for renal cell carcinoma (RCC) characterization, yet clinical interpretation relies on subjective visual comparison across phases. We present PRISM (Phase-Resolved Isotropic Subtraction Mapping), an open-source automated pipeline that transforms multi-phase CT acquisitions into registered digital subtraction angiography (DSA) volumes with color-coded enhancement maps.
