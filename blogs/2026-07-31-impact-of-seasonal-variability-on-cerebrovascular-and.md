The burden of cardiovascular and cerebrovascular diseases is rising rapidly across sub-Saharan Africa. Increasing scientific reports indicate that meteorological seasonality may be associated with vascular hospitalization, yet evidence from tropical West Africa remains scarce. Understanding how climate and seasonal factors influence vascular health and hospital admissions is essential for informing targeted, context-specific health system responses.
