Hepatitis B is a leading preventable cause of liver cancer, and in high-burden settings most chronic infection is acquired around birth. Nigeria carries Africas largest hepatitis B burden. Preventing mother-to-child transmission requires a chain of antenatal steps: testing pregnant women, acting on the result, and vaccinating the newborn.
