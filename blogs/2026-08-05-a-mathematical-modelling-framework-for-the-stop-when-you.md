To prevent antimicrobial resistance (AMR), antibiotic courses are traditionally prescribed to completion, overlooking their collateral impact on the commensal microbiota. We developed a deterministic within-host model linking pathogen growth, immune responses, antibiotic action, and commensal dynamics to explore how treatment duration influences resistance development.
