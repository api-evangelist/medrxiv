Many disease-associated variants are thought to act through gene regulation, yet conventional eQTL mapping explains only a fraction of GWAS loci, potentially because regulatory effects vary across cellular states and environments. We present CASTIE, a scalable Poisson mixed-model framework that directly models sparse single-cell read counts and enables genome-wide testing of genotype-by-context interactions without pre-screening for static effects.
