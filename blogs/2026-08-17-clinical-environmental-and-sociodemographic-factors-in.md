IntroductionEthnic minority populations are disproportionately affected by type 2 diabetes (T2D). We investigated ethnic differences in the risks of diabetes-related complications and mortality in the Netherlands, and identified clinical, sociodemographic and environmental determinants associated with these differences. MethodsWe included 175,112 adults with T2D from the dynamic prospective primary care cohort DIAMANT.
