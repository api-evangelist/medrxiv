BackgroundClinical trials demonstrated high efficacy of daily and event-driven oral pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) in HIV prevention. Event-driven PrEP involves taking two tablets before and two times one tablet after sexual contact (2-1-1/on-demand). While both are implemented in Dutch clinical practice, evaluating real-world effectiveness requires large-scale data from routine clinical care.
