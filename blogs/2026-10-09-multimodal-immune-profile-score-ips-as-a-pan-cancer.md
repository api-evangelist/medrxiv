Background Immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI) therapies have revolutionized oncology, but identifying patients who benefit remains a challenge, particularly in populations where ICIs are not the standard of care, including microsatellite stable colorectal cancer (MSS CRC) and rare solid cancers. We evaluated real-world performance of the Immune Profile Score (IPS), a validated DNA- and RNA-based molecular signature, in these cohorts.
