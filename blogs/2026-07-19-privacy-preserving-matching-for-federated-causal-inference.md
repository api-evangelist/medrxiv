Causal effect estimates can often be biased in clinical and epidemiological studies as patient cohorts frequently exhibit substantial covariate imbalances between treated and control groups, often amplified in multicentre studies due to heterogeneous recruitment, clinical practice, and case mix. Covariate balancing methods are therefore essential for valid causal inference.
