Bainbridge-Ropers syndrome (BRS) is a rare neurodevelopmental disorder caused by truncating mutations in the epigenetic regulator ASXL3. While traditionally considered a haploinsufficiency disorder, the precise molecular mechanisms driving BRS remain poorly understood. Here, we combine patient-derived cellular lines and novel mouse models to elucidate the molecular function of disease-associated ASXL3 variants.
