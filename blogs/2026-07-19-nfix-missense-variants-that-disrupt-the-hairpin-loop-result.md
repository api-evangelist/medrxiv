Purpose: Pathogenic variants in NFIX cause Marshall-Smith syndrome and Malan syndrome (MALNS). We identified a severe subtype of MALNS characterized by adolescent-onset musculoskeletal deterioration and investigated functional consequences of underlying variants. Methods: Clinical data were collected from seven individuals with pathogenic NFIX variants.
