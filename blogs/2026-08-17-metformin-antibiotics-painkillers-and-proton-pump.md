BackgroundEvidence indicates that commonly prescribed drugs can alter gut microbiome composition in the short term, whereas their long-term effects remain poorly understood. Understanding these drug-microbiome interactions is important for a wide range of gut-related diseases, including colorectal cancer (CRC), and for the development of microbiome-based CRC biomarkers. ObjectiveTo examine associations between prescription drug use, the gut microbiome and screening-detected colorectal lesions, and to investigate whether drug exposure and its timing influences microbial CRC screening biomarker
