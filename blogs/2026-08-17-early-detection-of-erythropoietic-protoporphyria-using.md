Objective: Erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP) is a rare photodermatosis marked by multi-year diagnostic delays. We developed and externally validated machine learning models to identify patients with EPP earlier from longitudinal electronic health record (EHR) data and estimate undiagnosed disease burden. Materials and Methods: In a retrospective case-control study at two San Francisco health systems, an academic referral center (UCSF) and a safety-net hospital (ZSFG) we identified 74 confirmed EPP cases using combined diagnostic coding, biochemical criteria, and specialty chart review.
