Background & Aims: Immune-mediated liver injury from immune checkpoint inhibitors (ILICI) is a major immune-related adverse event that limits cancer immunotherapy, yet its tissue-level immunobiology is poorly defined and its management is largely extrapolated from autoimmune hepatitis (AIH). We previously identified a tri-cellular CD8+ T cell-macrophage-hepatocyte injury niche in a murine model of ILICI; here, we tested whether this niche is recapitulated in human disease.
