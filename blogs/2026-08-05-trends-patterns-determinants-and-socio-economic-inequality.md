Background: Globally and in Bangladesh, high-risk fertility behaviour (HRFB) continues to be a significant public health issue, contributing to negative health outcomes for both mothers and children. So, this study aimed to evaluate the trends, prevalence, determinants, and socio-economic inequalities associated with HRFB among presently married women of reproductive age by utilising data from the Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey (BDHS). Methods: We analysed data from 19,060 currently married women aged 15-49 years.
