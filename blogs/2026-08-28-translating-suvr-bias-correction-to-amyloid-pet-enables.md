Introduction: A new method that corrects for time-dependent bias in standardized-uptake value ratios (SUVRs) was adapted and optimized for [11C]PiB (PiB) amyloid-beta (A{beta}) PET, across low-to-high A{beta} loads, relying only on PET data collected during the SUVR time-window. This modeling approach was evaluated in cross-sectional and longitudinal cohorts for earlier and shorter SUVR time-windows (30-45 min, 45-60 min) than commonly applied, to enable higher throughput imaging.
