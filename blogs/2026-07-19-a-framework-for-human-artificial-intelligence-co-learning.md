Objective To develop and evaluate a framework for human-AI interaction. This approach, SHARE (Synergistic Human-Agent REasoning system) was designed to support scalable phenotyping of complex outcomes accurately, robustly and reproducibly from real-world electronic health record (EHR) data to support real-world evidence (RWE) generation. Methods and Analysis Using rheumatoid arthritis (RA) disease activity as the use-case, we studied a multi-institutional EHR-based RA cohort of 3,167 patients.
