Background Urine tenofovir (uTFV) and dried blood spot (DBS) tenofovir diphosphate (TFV-DP) concentrations respectively estimate short- and medium-term adherence to tenofovir disoproxil fumarate (TDF)-based antiretroviral therapy (ART). We evaluated the accuracy of a point-of-care uTFV assay, and associations between uTFV/TFV-DP, and viral load (VL) and retention outcomes within a South African community ART programme. Methods We measured uTFV and DBS TFV-DP concentrations using liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS).
