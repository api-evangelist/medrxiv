Introduction Adolescent idiopathic scoliosis (AIS) requires frequent x-rays for management, exposing young patients to cumulative radiation risks. While radiation-sparing imaging modalities exist, access across the National Health Service (NHS) remains uneven and information given to patients is variable. This qualitative study investigated the systemic, geographic, and interpersonal dynamics of AIS imaging in England.
