Cytosolic phospholipase A2 (cPLA2), encoded by PLA2G4A plays a crucial role in platelet activation through thromboxane A2 biosynthesis. Defects in this pathway can lead to bleeding disorders; however, the precise role of cPLA2 in Glanzmann thrombasthenia (GT) or GT-like syndromes remains poorly understood. The current study emerged from a fortuitous discovery of novel PLA2G4A variants, p.Asp455Val (D455V) and p.Glu119Lys (E119K), among 3 GT patients from two unrelated families in south India.
