Multiple external factors shape human microbial communities. Beverages represent particularly frequent dietary exposures that may impact oral microbiome composition. We examined associations of coffee, tea, alcohol, sugar-sweetened beverages, artificially-sweetened beverages, and fruit juice consumption with the oral microbiome in three large cohort studies (n=5,598).
