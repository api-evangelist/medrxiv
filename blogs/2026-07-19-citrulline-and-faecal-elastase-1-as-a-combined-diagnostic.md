Background: Early detection of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) is critical. While faecal elastase-1 (FE-1) is a standard clinical marker for pancreatic function, its diagnostic accuracy for malignancy is limited. We sought to identify plasma metabolites that enhance FE-1 performance in symptomatic "at-risk" patients.
