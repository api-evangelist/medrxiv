Background: Electrocardiographic markers of atrial cardiomyopathy (AtCM) are associated with adverse cardiovascular outcomes. Whether AtCM further refines cardiovascular disease (CVD) mortality risk across increasing cardiometabolic risk factor (CMRF) burden is unclear. Methods: We analyzed 7,083 adults free of baseline CVD.
