Background Mortality in sepsis is often considered a direct consequence of infection and organ dysfunction, although many deaths occur after withdrawal of life sustaining treatment. The clinical factors leading to these decisions and their implications for interpreting sepsis mortality are not well characterized. Methods We performed a secondary analysis of a prospective observational cohort of adults with physician adjudicated sepsis at a tertiary academic medical center.
