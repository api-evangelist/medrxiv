Transcranial magnetic stimulation can provide noninvasive measures of cortical excitability and plasticity, but relationships between these measures and clinically observable features of autism are not fully characterized. Nineteen autistic participants aged 15 to 40 years were included in the analysis of a left primary motor cortex continuous theta-burst stimulation (cTBS) biomarker protocol. Motor evoked potentials were measured at baseline and at seven assessments from 5 to 60 min after stimulation.
