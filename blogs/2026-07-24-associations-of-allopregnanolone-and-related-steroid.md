Psychosocial distress is associated with adverse perinatal outcomes, yet its biological correlates remain incompletely understood. We evaluated associations of prenatal psychosocial distress with allopregnanolone (ALLO) and related steroids, and assessed race/ethnicity as a potential effect modifier, using data from 237 participants from the Healthy Start Study. We selected participants based on high distress (Edinburgh Perinatal Depression Scale [EPDS] >=13 or EPDS-3A >=7; n = 57) or low distress (EPDS <4 and EPDS-3A <2; n = 180).
