Objective: To develop and validate a supervised text-embedded transformer matching model to identify fall injuries in Medicare data, and evaluate the model's performance -- alongside a validated rule-based algorithm-- against "ground truth" from an external reference standard (self-reported fall injuries leading to medical attention). Materials and Methods: Text embeddings of ICD-10-CM and CPT codes in Medicare claims/encounters from participants in the Strategies to Reduce Injuries and Develop Confidence in Elders (STRIDE) trial served as model inputs.
