Background. Clinical quality measurement often relies on manual abstraction of medical records, an approach that is costly, burdensome, and often infeasible for measures requiring interpretation of narrative text; these constraints have shaped measure development itself, filtering out clinically important measures that are too difficult to operationalize. We evaluated whether neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence (NSAI), which combines large language model extraction with symbolic reasoning, could reliably abstract complex quality measures from narrative pathology reports.
