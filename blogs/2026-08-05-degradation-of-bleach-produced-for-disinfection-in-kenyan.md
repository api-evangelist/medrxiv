Disinfection with bleach is recommended for surface cleaning in healthcare facilities, but bleach degrades over time, reducing disinfection effectiveness. We evaluated whether local bleach produced by hospitals using novel technology had adequate shelf life to justify use. Two hospitals (A and B) each produced and stored 0.5% bleach in two 20-liter plastic containers; bleach in one container was alkalinized for increased stability to pH 12; bleach in the other container was not stabilized and, for comparison, a third container of commercial bleach was tested.
