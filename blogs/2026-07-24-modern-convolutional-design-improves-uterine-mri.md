Purpose: To evaluate how segmentation architecture and dataset-adaptive configuration influence uterine MRI segmentation across heterogeneous benign and malignant tasks. Methods: U-Net, Swin-UNETR, and MedNeXt were compared with nnU-Net as a self-configuring reference across T2-weighted MRI datasets: public multiclass UMD anatomy/fibroid segmentation (n=300), institutional endometrial cancer tumor segmentation (n=206), and institutional uterine mass lesion segmentation (n=234). A relabeled external UMD-style cohort (n=12) assessed domain shift.
