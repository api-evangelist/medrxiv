High low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) and triglyceride (TG) levels are associated with heavy alcohol consumption, but the accuracy of LDL-C estimation equations according to drinking status remains unclear. We analyzed direct LDL-C and drinking-status data from 28,882 participants in the Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey, classified as non/ex-drinkers, moderate drinkers, or binge drinkers.
