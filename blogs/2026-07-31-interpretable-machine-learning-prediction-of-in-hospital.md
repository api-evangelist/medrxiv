Background: Critically ill patients with cancer and sepsis have high in-hospital mortality, but externally validated prediction models are limited. Objective: To develop and externally validate an interpretable machine learning framework using first-day intensive care data. Methods: We used MIMIC-IV version 3.1 for development and internal validation and eICU-CRD for external validation.
