Objectives Genetic testing is increasingly used in the diagnosis and management of respiratory conditions, including pulmonary fibrosis (PF). The perspectives of people with PF and healthcare professionals (HCP) on the use of genetic testing remain largely unexplored. Methods A qualitative study was undertaken.
