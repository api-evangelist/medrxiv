ImportanceWhile increased physical activity (PA) and decreased sedentary behavior (SB) are associated with favorable health outcomes, evidence regarding their causal effects on healthcare costs remains limited. ObjectiveTo assess the causal effects of PA and SB on healthcare costs. DesignA two-sample Mendelian randomization (MR) study.
