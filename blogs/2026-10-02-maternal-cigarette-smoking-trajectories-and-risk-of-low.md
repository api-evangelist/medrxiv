Background: Maternal cigarette smoking is associated with adverse birth outcomes, but low birthweight may reflect preterm delivery, restricted fetal growth, or both. Contemporary evidence distinguishing smoking trajectories is limited. Objectives: To examine associations between maternal cigarette-smoking trajectories and low birthweight among U.S.-resident singleton live births in 2023 to 2024.
