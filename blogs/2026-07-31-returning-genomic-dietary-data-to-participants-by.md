Returning individual results to research participants is increasingly expected in genomic studies. Yet, genomic dietary data have unique characteristics. New DNA sequencing techniques reconstruct diet from the residual plant and animal DNA recoverable in stool, reporting not nutrients or calories but a list of the species a person ate.
