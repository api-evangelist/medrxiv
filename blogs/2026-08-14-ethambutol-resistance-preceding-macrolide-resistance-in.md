Objectives: To investigate whether ethambutol resistance in Mycobacterium avium complex is associated with the emergence of macrolide resistance. Methods: Patients who developed macrolide resistance during guideline-based treatment were included, and longitudinal analyses of minimum inhibitory concentrations and mutations in embB or the upstream region of embA were performed. Clinical, microbiological, and radiological characteristics were compared according to the mutation status of embB or embA upstream region, prior to the emergence of macrolide resistance.
