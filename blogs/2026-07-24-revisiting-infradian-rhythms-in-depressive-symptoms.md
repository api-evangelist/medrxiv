Circadian biology has long been implicated in mood and depressive disorders. While longer, infradian rhythms are increasingly recognised across diverse physiological processes, their relevance to mood remains poorly understood. Using long-term wearable heart rate data, this study mapped depressive symptom severity onto person-specific multiday physiological phase.
