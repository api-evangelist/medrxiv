Background: Clonal haematopoiesis (CH), the age-related expansion of mutated blood stem cells, is detectable in around 10% of people in their seventies, rises steeply with age, and is associated with several diseases. Its relationship with neurodegeneration remains contentious, with conflicting evidence for both protective and detrimental effects on Alzheimer's disease (AD).
