Selenoproteins are a specialised group of proteins that incorporate selenium, an essential micronutrient, in the form of selenocysteine. The SECIS binding protein 2 (SBP2), encoded by SECISBP2, is a crucial component of the selenocysteine incorporation machinery. SECISBP2 deficiency compromises selenoprotein synthesis, and its knockout causes embryonic lethality in mice.
