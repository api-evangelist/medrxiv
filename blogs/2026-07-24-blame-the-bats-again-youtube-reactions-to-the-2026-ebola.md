The World Health Organization declared the 2026 outbreak of Ebola disease caused by Bundibugyo virus in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Uganda a Public Health Emergency of International Concern. Public engagement on video-sharing platforms such as YouTube offers insight into public perceptions during such crises, particularly in the affected region, yet these reactions remain largely uncharacterised.
