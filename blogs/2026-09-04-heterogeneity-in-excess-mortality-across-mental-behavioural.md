Importance: Excess mortality associated with mental disorders is well established, but estimates are largely based on specialist psychiatric populations. Whether they characterize mortality in the broader diagnosed population is uncertain. Objective: To characterize heterogeneity in excess mortality by diagnosis, psychiatric care setting, substance use disorder (SUD), and time since diagnosis.
