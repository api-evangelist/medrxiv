Background Evidence on {beta}-thalassaemia carrier prevalence is fragmented across heterogeneous surveys and national summaries, leaving within-country variation and geographic concentration of carriers poorly resolved. This study characterises {beta}-thalassaemia carrier prevalence and carrier populations at high spatial resolution and identifies where additional data would most improve geographic coverage.
