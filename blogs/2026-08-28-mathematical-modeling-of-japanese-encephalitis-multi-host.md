Japanese encephalitis virus (JEV) transmission involves complex interactions among Culex mosquitoes, amplifying pig hosts, reservoir wading birds, humans, and environmental conditions, complicating quantitative assessment of transmission dynamics and intervention effectiveness. We developed a deterministic, fourteen-compartment One Health mathematical framework that integrates these interconnected host vector populations and their epidemiological states.
