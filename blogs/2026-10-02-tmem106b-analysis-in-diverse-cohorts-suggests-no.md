Recent evidence suggests that TMEM106B expression is reduced in the brains of patients with Parkinson's disease (PD). However, an association between rare genetic variants in TMEM106B and PD risk has not been previously established. In this work, we investigated the association between rare TMEM106B variants and PD risk.
