Background and Aims: Steps are increasingly used to prescribe physical activity, but their impact on quality of life (QoL) remains unclear. We examined the dose-response association between step metrics and QoL, and whether cardiovascular disease (CVD) status moderates this association. Methods: Individual-level data of five studies were pooled.
