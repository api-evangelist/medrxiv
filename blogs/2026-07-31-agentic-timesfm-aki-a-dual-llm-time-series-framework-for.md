Background: Acute kidney injury (AKI) is a severe complication in intensive care units, frequently exacerbated by synergistic nephrotoxicity from drugs such as Vancomycin and Piperacillin-Tazobactam. Traditional alert systems relying on static thresholds suffer from high false-positive rates and delayed detection. Methods: We developed Agentic-TimesFM-AKI, a dual-model architecture integrating a Large Language Model (Gemma-4 Sentinel) with a zero-shot time-series forecaster (TimesFM) to provide continuous, dynamic risk forecasting and transparent clinical reasoning.
