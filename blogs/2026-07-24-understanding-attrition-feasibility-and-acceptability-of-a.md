INTRODUCTION: Behavioral intervention studies for family care partners for those with dementia need to be both feasible and acceptable in order implement and complete the investigative work. Our study, Tele-STELLA (Support via Technology: Living and Learning with Advancing dementia), was completed in 2025. While 188 care partners enrolled, the attrition rate was high (44%).
