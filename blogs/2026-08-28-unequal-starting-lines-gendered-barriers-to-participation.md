Adolescents with perinatally acquired HIV carry a high burden of depression and anxiety, and where specialist mental health services are scarce, peer-led physical activity has been proposed as a low-cost supportive intervention. Whether such programs reach girls and boys equally, and whether gendered constraints shape who is able to take part, has received little attention.
