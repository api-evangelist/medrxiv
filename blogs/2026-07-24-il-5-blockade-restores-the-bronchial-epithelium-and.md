Rationale Airway remodelling (AR) contributes to airflow limitation and poor symptom control in severe asthma. While anti-IL-5 therapy improves clinical outcomes in severe asthma, the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying its effects on AR remain incompletely understood. Objectives.
