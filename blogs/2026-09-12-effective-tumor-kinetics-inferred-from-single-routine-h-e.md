Routine H&E-histopathology captures spatial snapshots of tumor ecosystems, yet computational pathology largely treats them as textures without explicit links to underlying kinetics. We show that patient-specific parameters of a mechanistic reaction-diffusion model can be inferred from a single biopsy. Cell-nucleus point patterns are fitted jointly to the analytical two-point correlation function and power spectral density, recovering patient-specific mechanistic proliferation and diffusion rates across eleven multicenter cohorts.
