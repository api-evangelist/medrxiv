Accelerated 5x5 repetitive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (rTMS; five stimulation sessions per day for five days) is an effective treatment for Major Depressive Disorder (MDD), and has efficacy comparable to conventional once-daily rTMS. Considering the heterogeneity of symptoms in patients with MDD, it is critical to determine how accelerated 5x5 and conventional rTMS affect depression symptom domains.
