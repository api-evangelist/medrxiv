Background: Venous thromboembolism (VTE), including deep vein thrombosis (DVT), remains a major global health burden. Diagnostic pathways rely on ultrasound but are limited by availability and prolonged time-to-imaging. Novel artificial intelligence (AI) guidance systems have been designed to enable non-ultrasound-trained operators to acquire proximal lower extremity compression ultrasounds for remote clinician interpretation.
