Fracture nonunion remains a major cause of morbidity, yet patient-specific factors associated with impaired healing remain incompletely characterized. Anemia has been associated with adverse orthopaedic outcomes, but its relationship with fracture nonunion is poorly understood. We examined whether pre-fracture anemia, anemia burden, and clinically relevant anemia subtypes were associated with nonunion following tibial or femoral fractures.
