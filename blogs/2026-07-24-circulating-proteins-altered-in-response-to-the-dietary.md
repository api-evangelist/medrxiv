The Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension (DASH) diet reduces blood pressure and cholesterol. However, the mechanisms underlying these effects are unclear, and no randomized studies have evaluated the long-term benefits on health outcomes such as coronary artery disease (CAD) or type 2 diabetes (T2D). We performed a series of Mendelian randomization analyses of 71 serum proteins perturbed by the DASH diet in previous randomized controlled feeding studies to understand their potential mechanistic role on health outcomes.
