Abstract Rationale: Host genotypes can predict subtherapeutic anti-tubercular drug exposures and treatment-associated toxicities. Screening for these variants could enable personalized dosing, but scalable assays for second-line drugs are lacking. Objectives: We developed a nanopore sequencing panel to detect host variants affecting anti-tuberculosis drug troughs and toxicities, and evaluated its performance as a saliva-based screening tool.
