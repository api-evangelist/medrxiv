*Background:** Hospital-acquired venous thromboembolism (VTE) is a leading preventable cause of in-hospital morbidity and mortality. Guideline-endorsed risk scores (Padua, IMPROVE) achieve only moderate discrimination in unselected hospital-wide cohorts. **Methods:** We analyzed 399,624 adult admissions in MIMIC-IV (2008-2022), excluding admissions with prior VTE to restrict the cohort to first-ever disease.
