Objectives: To investigate whether genetic predisposition to hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (HDP), measured using polygenic risk scores (PRS), is associated with subsequent cardiovascular disease (CVD) outcomes in analyses stratified by HDP history. Study design and main outcome measures: This population-based cohort included 1,284 women from the 1994-95 Busselton Health Study with genotype data, pregnancy history, cardiovascular risk factors, and linked hospitalisation and death records to 2022.
