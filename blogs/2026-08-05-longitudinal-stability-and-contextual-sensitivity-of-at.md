At-home collected salivary Dim Light Melatonin (DLMO) assessments have demonstrated agreement with laboratory-determined DLMO estimates, but key translational gaps remain. We compiled 32 at home salivary DLMO assessments obtained over four years from a healthy adult male (38 to 42 years) with stable sleep-wake patterns to characterize the magnitude of behavioral, environmental, and pharmacologic influences on DLMO and melatonin profile morphology at the individual level.
