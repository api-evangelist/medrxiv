Background and Objective. Missing data frequently occurs in health databases and can bias analyses if not correctly dealt with. Using real-world data, we compared complete-case and multiple-imputation methods for recovering true parameters of a multivariable logistic regression model for the association between maternal glucose levels during pregnancy and child excess weight at preschool age, where missing values were present in as much as 30% of our sample.
