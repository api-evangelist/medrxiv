Antiretroviral therapy (ART) stock-outs interrupt treatment, increase the risk of virologic failure and drug resistance, and erode the population-level benefits of viral suppression. India's National AIDS Control Organization (NACO) manages one of the world's largest public ART programmes, where regimen transitions, evolving formulations, changing treatment guidelines, and procurement-driven fluctuations in drug consumption complicate forecasting. We developed an end-to-end, regimen-specific forecasting workflow to support procurement planning during such periods of instability.
