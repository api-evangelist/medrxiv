Purpose: To build a 3D U-Net model, BioDeformUNet, to predict the deformation vector field (DVF) of the liver in near real-time, for efficient intra-procedural evaluation of the minimal ablative margin (MAM). Materials and Methods: This retrospective study included 170 contrast-enhanced computed tomography (CECT) image pairs from 157 patients who underwent liver ablation treatment between 2020-2024. Each data instance included one pre-ablation CECT (pre-CECT) and one post-ablation CECT (post-CECT).
