Background. Hematoporphyrin derivative (HpD, Hiporfin) is used for photodynamic therapy (PDT) of solid tumors but had never been evaluated for fluorescence guided resection (FGR) in glioma. We assessed Hiporfin fluorescence accuracy against histopathology in recurrent glioblastoma (rGBM) and the feasibility of adding cavity PDT to FGR.
