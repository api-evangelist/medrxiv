Vertebral artery stenosis is a condition amenable to treatment with stenting. To accurately size stents, angiography is the most used imaging technique despite its limitation as a lumenogram with few planes of imagery. The technique's deficiencies are especially apparent at the vertebral artery origin due to image obstruction by surrounding vessels as well as vessel tortuosity.
