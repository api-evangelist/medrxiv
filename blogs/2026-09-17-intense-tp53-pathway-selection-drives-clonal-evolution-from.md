ERCC6L2 disease (ED) is an inherited bone marrow failure (BMF) syndrome that progresses almost exclusively to erythroid, TP53-mutated myeloid malignancy, but the somatic evolution underlying the progression in patients is unknown. We characterized the genetic landscape of 29 ED patients with longitudinal sampling, integrating whole-genome and single-cell RNA sequencing, and compared the findings with Shwachman-Diamond syndrome (SDS), another TP53 mutation-prone disease, to define somatic evolution across disease stages.
