BackgroundChronic kidney disease of undetermined cause (CKDu) is a form of kidney disease not associated with traditional risk factors such as hypertension, diabetes or heavy proteinuria. 11.2% of men and 3.7% of women demonstrated low eGFR (a surrogate for CKDu) in the absence of these risk factors in a 2017 cross-sectional population-representative survey of adults in North Central Province, Sri Lanka. We therefore established a longitudinal cohort to track changes in kidney function over time and to identify risk factors for developing poor kidney health.
