Background Breast cancer diagnosed at a younger age tends to be more aggressive and have worse outcomes. While rare pathogenic variants in multiple susceptibility genes and >200 common variants have been identified for breast cancer, >50% of the familial risk of early-onset breast cancer (EOBC) remains unexplained. Little is known about the EOBC non-genetic risk factors.
