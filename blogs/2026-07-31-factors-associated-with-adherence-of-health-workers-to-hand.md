Background Hand hygiene is the most effective and cost-efficient measure for preventing healthcare-associated infections (HAIs). However, adherence among healthcare workers remains suboptimal, particularly in low-resource settings. Purpose/Aim This study assessed the level of adherence to hand hygiene practices and the factors associated with adherence among healthcare workers at Lubaga Hospital, Kampala City.
