Background: Sleep, pain, affect, cognition and energy (SPACE) has been described as a latent symptom-severity construct in chronic overlapping pain conditions. Its population-level structure and associations with chronic pain interference and functional disability remain uncertain. Methods: We conducted a cross-sectional analysis of UK Biobank participants.
